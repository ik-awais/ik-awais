## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/QssGEmpkyEOhBCb7e1/giphy.gif" width="25" alt="About icon" /> About

I am an Operations Manager at **[AI GenMat](https://aigenmat.com)**, working at the intersection of operations and technology. My work focuses on improving workflows, strengthening processes, and implementing practical AI-enabled solutions that make day-to-day operations smoother, more stable, and easier to scale.

Alongside my professional work, I am pursuing a **BS in Software Engineering at FAST-NUCES Islamabad**. My academic journey began with BS Artificial Intelligence at FAST-NUCES Peshawar. My professional journey at AI GenMat has included HR operations, company leadership, and operations management.
